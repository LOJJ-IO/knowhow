Why pills, not dots

Shared-green and not-shared-red sit at ΔE 4.9 under deuteranopia — indistinguishable for roughly 1 in 12 men. The state is therefore never carried by colour alone.

Typography

Plus Jakarta Sans carries headings and numerals; Public Sans carries UI and body. Both fall back to Helvetica/Arial, which is what the app renders today — so exports and no-webfont environments degrade gracefully.
